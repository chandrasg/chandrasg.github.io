Three original OSF version-1 files informed the analysis: a Regulations.gov comments CSV, preprocessing HTML, and topic/theme/domain mapping workbook. The original bytes were verified by SHA256 and exact length. On September 8, 2026, the OSF node API returned no license or node-license value and no license relationship. Public accessibility is not treated as blanket permission to redistribute third-party comments or workbook contents.

This website publishes independently computed factual aggregate counts and topic–theme relationships, with short taxonomy labels and numerical targets attributed to the article and original author workbook. It changes presentation and adds overlap/date views. The original model, annotation meanings and source discrepancies are not revised.

Original source files, individual comments, selected examples, personal or record metadata, commenter identifiers, source scripts and text-bearing workbooks are excluded. The interactive page makes no new data collection or model/API inference calls. The historical preprocessing HTML was inspected but not executed.
