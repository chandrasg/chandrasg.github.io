ABOUT THIS INTERACTIVE PAPER

Study: Golos AM, Guntuku SC, Buttenheim AM (2024). “Do not inject our babies”: a social listening analysis of public opinion about authorizing pediatric COVID-19 vaccines. Health Affairs Scholar, 2(7), qxae082.
https://doi.org/10.1093/haschl/qxae082

WHAT THE PAGE SHOWS
The page adapts the three-domain framework in Figure 1 and displays the 13 theme counts printed in Tables 1–3. All counts refer to model topics. Topic assignments overlap; counts are not numbers or percentages of commenters. Theme explanations summarize the paper’s Results, and the communication implications summarize its Discussion.

The study analyzed 149,897 comments after exact deduplication. The authors selected a 100-topic model and retained 95 topics for interpretation, organizing them into 13 themes and three domains: What, Why, and How.

HOW TO EXPLORE
Select a domain to focus on specific concerns, underlying beliefs, or persuasive strategies. Select a theme to read a plain-language explanation. The download contains the 13 published counts and their source tables.

INTERPRETATION
These self-selected comments were submitted to two FDA regulatory dockets in October 2021 and February 2022. The study characterizes discourse around vaccine opposition; it is not a representative opinion survey. Theme labels describe arguments and rhetoric, not evidence of vaccine effects. The paper did not test the effectiveness of communication interventions.
