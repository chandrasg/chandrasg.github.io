03 / What to take from the study

Evaluate language models across the people they serve

For both language representations, models had weaker correlations with depression scores in the Black test group—even when trained on Black participants. The findings highlight why performance in one group should not stand in for evaluation across communities.

The study matched Black and White English-speaking adults in the United States on age and gender; 76% of participants were women. Participants reported symptoms using PHQ-9 and consented to share Facebook updates. The results concern this sample, platform and set of language features. They do not establish why the differences arose or imply that depression cannot be expressed in language in any group.

This page explains group-level research findings. It does not assess an individual’s mental health.
